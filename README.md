
# Blinkit Sales Analysis Dashboard | Microsoft Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

The Blinkit Sales Analysis Dashboard is an interactive Excel dashboard developed to analyze retail sales performance, product categories, outlet characteristics, and customer ratings.

The project transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights to support sales monitoring, performance analysis, and data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze total sales and key performance indicators (KPIs).
- Identify top-performing product categories.
- Compare sales across outlet sizes and location tiers.
- Analyze sales by fat content and item type.
- Track sales trends by outlet establishment year.
- Develop an interactive dashboard for business reporting.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel
- Data Analysis
- Data Visualization
- Excel Dashboard Development
- KPI Reporting
- Business Intelligence

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales Performance Analysis
- Total sales and average sales
- Number of items and average rating
- Sales trends by outlet establishment year
- Sales comparison by outlet type

### 2. Product Category Analysis
- Sales by item type
- Low-fat versus regular product sales
- Fat content analysis by outlet
- Comparison of product category performance

### 3. Outlet Analysis
- Sales by outlet size: High, Medium, and Small
- Sales by location tier: Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3
- Sales and average sales by outlet type
- Number of items by outlet type

### 4. Interactive Filters
- Outlet Size
- Outlet Location Type
- Item Type

These filters allow users to explore sales performance across different product and outlet categories.

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Total sales are approximately $1.20 million.
- Regular-fat products contribute a larger share of sales than low-fat products.
- Fruits and Vegetables and Snack Foods are among the leading item categories by sales.
- Tier 3 outlets record the highest sales among the displayed location tiers.
- Medium-sized outlets contribute the largest sales share among the displayed outlet sizes.
- Supermarket Type1 has the highest total sales among the displayed outlet types.
- The outlet establishment trend reaches its highest displayed sales value around 2018.
